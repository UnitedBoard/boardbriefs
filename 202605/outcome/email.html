Board Briefs
 
May 2026
 
 
 

Dear Trustees,

I am deeply grateful for your partnership and witness as we continue this shared journey of advancing whole person education across Asia.

The pages that follow offer a glimpse of the period since the last issue of Board Briefs in March, marked by remarkable momentum and meaningful engagement across our network. You will read about our groundbreaking work in Kerala and Tamil Nadu, where over 50 academic leaders and faculty from 18 institutions gathered for transformative professional development—our largest regional engagement to date. The depth of connection and renewed sense of purpose expressed by participants reminds us why this work matters.

Our commitment to campus ministry found powerful expression through the 2026 Asian Academy for Campus Ministry in Chiang Mai, where leaders from 11 countries explored mental health, spiritual development, and the calling to accompany students through complex, multifaith realities. The program's emphasis on belonging, accompaniment, and compassionate presence reflects the heart of our educational mission.

This issue also highlights first fruits from two of our digital initiatives: the Education Technology Conferences Grant, which supported faculty presentations at international conferences across four continents, and our AI Governance for Higher Education Institutions in Asia Workshop Series, which equipped 70 participants from 21 institutions to develop context-sensitive policies for responsible innovation. Together, these programs demonstrate how we are helping institutions navigate technological transformation with both imagination and accountability.

You will also find a report on our ASIANetwork forum in Illinois, which examined the evolving landscape of Asia-US higher education collaboration, and updates from my spring travels that deepened relationships with donors and friends across the United States. These connections sustain and energize our collective work.

It is with profound sorrow that we remember the passing of Tony Ruger, whose 16 years of faithful service, wise counsel, and compassionate leadership enriched the United Board immeasurably. His legacy endures in the institutions he strengthened and the lives he touched.

Finally, we warmly welcome Kansikorn Yusook (Pui) as Administrative Assistant in our Chiang Mai office, whose skills and experience will serve our Southeast Asia operations well.

Thank you for your steadfast commitment to this mission and to the educators, students, and institutions we serve together. Please note that the itinerary and schedule for our upcoming Board Meeting and Study Trip in India is appended at the end of this Issue.

 

With gratitude and warm regards,

 
 

Pareena Gupta Lawrence
President
United Board for Christian Higher Education in Asia

New Staff
 
 

Kansikorn Yusook, Administrative Assistant, Chiang Mai Office

We are pleased to welcome Ms. Kansikorn Yusook (Pui) who joined the United Board in May as Administrative Assistant at its Chiang Mai office.

Pui holds a Bachelor’s degree in International Business Administration from Rajamangala University of Technology Thanyaburi (RMUTT), Thailand. She has extensive experience coordinating local and international operations across the private sector, public institutions, and diplomatic environments. She previously served as Consular Secretary at the Austrian Consulate in Chiang Mai and worked with the National Innovation Agency (a public organization), supporting international startups entering Thailand.

She specializes in executive coordination, administrative operations, and stakeholder management, with expertise in operations management, HR administration, financial coordination, and marketing support. She has experience working across cross-functional teams and is recognized for her strong organizational skills, adaptability, and effectiveness in multicultural, fast-paced environments.

Pui’s responsibilities include providing administrative support to the President, overseeing daily office operations, supporting the Chiang Mai team, and coordinating logistics for United Board’s events and programs in Southeast Asia. We warmly welcome Pui into our United Board family.
