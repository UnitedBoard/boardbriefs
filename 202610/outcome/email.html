David Johns Delivers Commencement Address
at CMS College Kottayam

Dr. David L. Johns, Senior Consultant, was the Chief Guest at the Graduation Ceremony for the Class of 2026 at CMS College Kottayam (Autonomous) in Kerala, India, on September 3. The ceremony was presided over by the Rt. Rev. Dr. Malayil Sabu Koshy Cherian, Bishop of CSI Madhya Kerala Diocese and Manager of the College, with Principal Prof. Dr. Anju Sosan George in attendance.

In his commencement address, "A Life that Surprises You," David told the graduates that success amplifies whatever is already inside a person, so the more important question is who they are. He drew on his years as a university vice president and president to describe the responsibility that comes with influence. He also told the story of an unplanned stay in Honduras that changed the course of his life and career. He urged the graduates to cultivate two qualities, curiosity and hospitality, and linked them to the College's pledge to "grow in humility" and pursue the "ever widening horizon of insight." He closed by wishing them "a life much larger, much richer, and much fuller than you can possibly imagine."

David, based in the Chiang Mai office, focuses on leadership development, regional engagement, and cross-cultural program design. He was president of Ferrum College from 2018 to 2022, and he had already led faculty institutes at CMS College before this visit.

 

 

Welcoming New Staff

Christopher Butler, Director of Communications

Dr. Christopher Butler joined the United Board as Director of Communications in October 2026. He is based in the Chiang Mai office and leads the United Board's communications strategy, including publications, digital platforms, and brand stewardship. He works with colleagues across our offices to tell the story of whole person education and of our partner institutions across Asia.

Christopher has more than 20 years of experience in international and strategic communications. Before that, he spent a decade teaching sociology and English at the University of Minnesota, Morris. As Deputy Director of Communications and managing editor at the International Potato Center (CGIAR), he led a team that produced some 300 publications a year. He has also managed publications at the International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development (ICIMOD) in Nepal. Most recently, he was Senior Technical Director on a U.S. Department of State program in Ukraine. He holds a PhD in Sociology from the University of California, Santa Cruz.

 

Warunee Klinsukhon, Office Manager, Chiang Mai

Warunee Klinsukhon joined the United Board as Office Manager of the Chiang Mai office in August 2026. She is overseeing the setup and day-to-day running of the office, and she handles logistics for United Board programs and events in Thailand.

Warunee has nearly 15 years of experience in administration and human resources, most recently at Grace International School. Earlier in her career, she coordinated programs for NGOs and international organizations, including Compassion International (Thailand) and the Christian Conference of Asia. She holds a Bachelor's degree in English from Payap University in Chiang Mai.
