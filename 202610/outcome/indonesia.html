October 2026
 
 
 
 
 
Deepening Ties in Indonesia and Remembering Budi
 
 

From September 9 to 14, Pareena led a delegation to Indonesia that included Hope Antone, Christian Aung, Marcia France and Anita Tiphagne. Trustees and staff will recall the study trip of June 2024; this visit went further, reaching three additional network institutions and allowing the longer, deeper conversations a study tour rarely permits.

A particularly meaningful part of the visit came in Semarang, where Budi Widianarko's family received the delegation in his home. Budi liked to say that everyone has two professions—teacher and farmer—because whenever we share knowledge or help something grow, we add to the flourishing of the world. Pareena presented a Citation of Appreciation to his wife, Imevianty Lim, and their family, who spoke about the life behind his public service: his commitment to Indonesian higher education, to the United Board, and to the people around him. The delegation came to honor a colleague whom many on the Board continue to miss, and left with a better sense of the person he was.
 
At Universitas Atma Jaya Yogyakarta At Duta Wacana Christian University
 

The week also offered encouraging evidence that the United Board's programs take root. At Sanata Dharma University, the delegation hosted a day-long Faculty Development workshop that brought together 43 faculty members from six institutions. Pareena's keynote returned to first principles — why we teach, and for whom — before Hope and Marcia led sessions on Whole Person Education and teaching as a vocation, and Christian joined Dr. Oscar Karnalim of Maranatha Christian University for sessions on AI. When asked what they most needed to learn next, the participants did not put technology first: their priority was the ethical and responsible use of AI. They have already moved past asking whether to use it; their question is how to use it well. That evening, 41 program alumni gathered from across Indonesia, including United Board Fellows from the 2012 and 2026 cohorts. Particularly encouraging was the desire to give back that several alumni expressed.
 
Meeting APTIK At Sanata Dharma University
 

At Duta Wacana Christian University, we discussed how Christian higher education can serve a country so often hit by environmental crises, along with the university's interest in collaborating with institutions across the network. At Soegijapranata Catholic University, students were refreshingly direct about wanting more internships, projects and mentoring. Marcia and Anita were warmly received at Universitas Atma Jaya Yogyakarta by a room full of program alumni. In Yogyakarta the delegation also met the leadership of APTIK, an association of more than 27 Catholic universities — a promising first conversation about where the United Board's work overlaps. Satya Wacana Christian University continues its important work through a center for the study of religions and Christian–Muslim relations. At Petra Christian University, we delivered sessions on gender equity, faculty development, strategic planning and partnership, and Pareena addressed the weekly worship service.
 
At Petra Christian University At Satya Wacana Christian University
 

The week confirmed what Budi understood so well: the United Board's role is to teach where it can, and to tend and help grow what its partners are building.
