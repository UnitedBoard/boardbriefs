A Passage to India
 
 

The week of June 7 to 12 was filled with warm welcomes, candid conversations, and shared celebrations. Our annual study trip began in Chennai with an evening reception featuring a keynote presentation on the trends of Indian higher education, alumni testimonies, and a heartfelt farewell to Dr. Paul Appasamy, marked with a celebration of his birthday. The next day, we visited two of our longest-standing partners, Women's Christian College and Madras Christian College, where we also launched the new MCC–United Board Mentor Partner Program.

In Bengaluru, CHRIST (Deemed to be University) hosted us across its Kengeri and central campuses, where we met participants in the Leadership Hub program, and witnessed the launch of the Strategic Orientation for Academic Resilience (SOAR) initiative. The day was capped by student cultural performances and a gala dinner marking 25 years of partnership.

The trip ended in West Bengal, where we visited Salesian College at its Siliguri campus before travelling up into the Darjeeling hills for our Business Meeting and the rest of the itinerary. The photos that follow capture something of the hospitality, energy, and friendship that accompanied us at every stop.
